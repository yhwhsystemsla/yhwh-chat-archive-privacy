Privacy Policy

YHWH Chat Archive

YHWH Systems, LLC · Effective September 26, 2026

Purpose

YHWH Chat Archive is provided by YHWH Systems, LLC. Its single purpose is to create, maintain, search, export, and verify independent backups of ChatGPT conversations for users who choose to use the service.

Information processed

When enabled on supported ChatGPT pages, YHWH Chat Archive may process conversation text, conversation titles, message metadata, conversation identifiers, references to attachments or generated artifacts, and technical diagnostics needed to determine whether a backup completed successfully.

The extension also stores configuration necessary to connect to the user's authorized YHWH Chat Archive service, including the archive service origin, a device identifier, local queue and retry state, and a device signing key created during enrollment.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide archive, recovery, search, export, integrity-verification, diagnostics, and support functions requested by the user.

YHWH Systems, LLC does not use archived conversation content for advertising, does not sell archived conversation content or personal information, and does not use archived conversation content to make credit, lending, employment, insurance, or other unrelated eligibility decisions.

Where information is sent

The Chrome Web Store version of the extension transmits archive data only to the YHWH Chat Archive service at https://archive.yhwhsystems.com after the user grants permission to access that service.

The extension operates on supported ChatGPT origins, including chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com, only for the purpose of capturing conversations for archival.

Authentication and local secrets

Initial device enrollment uses a one-time bootstrap secret. After successful enrollment, that bootstrap secret is removed from extension storage. The extension generates and stores a device signing key locally in Chrome storage and uses it to authenticate archive requests.

Data retention

Archive retention is controlled by the retention policy selected for the user's YHWH Chat Archive service. Local queued captures are retained only as needed to deliver them reliably or surface a failed-capture condition.

Sharing and sale of data

YHWH Systems, LLC does not sell archived data. Data may be disclosed only to service providers acting on behalf of YHWH Systems where necessary to operate the archive service, when directed by the user or organization controlling the archive, or when required by applicable law.

Remote code

The extension does not download or execute remote JavaScript or WebAssembly. Executable extension code is packaged with the extension. Network responses are processed as data.

User control

Users can disable or uninstall the extension at any time. Authorized archive users can export archived data through the extension's archive interface. Requests concerning retained archive data can be directed to YHWH Systems support.
